Each CSV file is the output of a corresponding SQL query:

01_data_quality_check.csv
Checks for missing values and data inconsistencies across all variables.
Helps identify issues before performing further analysis.

02_data_exploration.csv
Provides initial descriptive statistics, including counts, distributions, and sample rows.
Useful for understanding the dataset structure.

03_vaccination_coverage.csv
Calculates overall vaccination coverage rates among surveyed children.
Includes counts and percentages of children who received each vaccine.

04_vaccination_coverage_by_region.csv
Disaggregates vaccination coverage by geographic regions.
Allows comparison of coverage across locations.

05_risk_factor_analysis.csv
Explores associations between child health outcomes and potential risk factors.
Flags children affected by identified risk factors.

06_vaccine_barriers_analysis.csv
Identifies children who faced barriers to receiving vaccinations.
Flags late or missed vaccinations and calculates proportions.

07_provider_barriers_analysis.csv
Flags children impacted by provider availability or treatment barriers.
Highlights access issues in the dataset.

08_barrier_overlap_analysis.csv
Identifies children experiencing both vaccine and provider/treatment barriers.
Useful for understanding overlapping vulnerabilities.
